Architecture Document Draft


System Design: A local RAG pipeline ensures privacy and follows technical constraints.

LLM Selection (Llama 3): Selected for high performance-to-size ratio and native support in Ollama.

Vector DB (ChromaDB/PGVector): ChromaDB was chosen for its lightweight local persistence and robust metadata filtering, which is essential for multi-user isolation.

Embedding Model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2): A standard open-source choice that balances fast inference (embedding time) with semantic accuracy.

Multi-User Approach: Implemented via Metadata Filtering. This ensures that no user can retrieve another’s data, meeting the "Security Requirements" without the overhead of maintaining dozens of separate databases.
